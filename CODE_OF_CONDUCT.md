# Code of conduct

Help make Handrail a considerate place to build tools that make everyday tasks easier.

Treat people respectfully, explain disagreements with specific evidence and welcome different abilities, backgrounds and levels of experience. Be patient with beginner questions and review contributions constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not disclose another person's private guide, page context or personal information without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, close disruptive discussions and limit participation when necessary. Enforcement should be proportionate and protect the people affected.

For a concern, ask the maintainer for a private contact route without posting sensitive details publicly. Security findings follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md). This policy applies to project repositories and project-managed community spaces.
